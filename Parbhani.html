02 / The problem

A grievance becomes harder to resolve
when access, ownership and follow-up are unclear.

ACCESS

“Where should I report my problem?”

Identifying the right office, explaining an issue repeatedly and travelling or calling can make seeking help difficult.

OWNERSHIP

“Who is responsible for taking action?”

A concern may involve the village, block or a district department. Each complaint needs a named official.

FOLLOW-UP

“What happened after I complained?”

Without a reference, updates and a way to question closure, the citizen has to keep pursuing the same concern.

The helpline connects access with accountability: one place to report, a responsible official to act and a visible path to resolution.

01

Understand and confirm

AI interprets text, transcribes audio and uses photos to clarify the issue. It confirms essential details with the citizen.

02

Register and assign

A reference links the grievance, village and responsible official. Uncertain or sensitive cases receive human review.

03

Update, escalate and close

Officials record action. Delays escalate through the approved hierarchy. Citizens get updates, give feedback and can reopen an unresolved issue.

07 / Monthly pricing approach

A bottom-up access model connects
village action with district oversight.

Monthly portal subscriptions follow the officials who use the system, from village level to block offices and district departments.

Billing unit: authorised officer / month

Coverage determines the required officer accounts. The district receives a consolidated subscription view.

Portal levelAccess and responsibilityMonthly billing basis
Village officialsLocal grievance queue, verification, action updates and closure evidence.Per authorised village official / month
Block officialsVillage oversight, reassignment, coordination and escalations.Per authorised block official / month
District officersDepartment queues, district reviews and authorised oversight for the Guardian Minister’s office.Per authorised district or review officer / month

No subscription is charged to citizens. Portal access expands with the agreed village, block and district officer coverage.

Final commercials reflect officer accounts, service scope and support. WhatsApp messaging and agreed AI usage are accounted for separately.
