Context & Gap

Mumbai’s citizen interface must move from fragmented access to intelligent orchestration.

The new baseline

Citizens expect immediate, mobile-first, language-friendly civic access without downloading separate apps or navigating complex portals.

  • WhatsApp-first behaviour: Citizens prefer a familiar chat interface for information, payments and status updates.
  • Multi-format interaction: Grievances now arrive as photos, voice notes, location pins and natural language.
  • Real-time expectation: Citizens expect acknowledgement, tracking and visible closure.

The operational gap

Disconnected channels create duplication, weak routing, limited accountability and poor visibility for leadership.

  • Service information is spread across departments and portals.
  • Grievance intake often lacks AI-assisted validation, deduplication and severity classification.
  • Ward-level heatmaps, sentiment and recurring issue patterns are not surfaced in real time.
  • Civic assets remain physically present but digitally passive.

05. Alerts & Broadcast Intelligence

Targeted civic communication instead of city-wide noise.

BMC can communicate proactively with citizens based on ward, locality, language, service category, emergency impact zone and citizen segment.

🚨 Emergency Alerts

Flood alerts, waterlogging, road closures, fire safety advisories, disease outbreaks and disaster warnings pushed to affected wards.

Example: G/South Ward — high-tide and waterlogging advisory.

📊 WhatsApp-to-Ward Sentiment

AI analyses citizen messages to detect frustration, satisfaction, recurring issues and local pressure points.

Example: Rising negative sentiment on garbage collection in one locality.

📡 Targeted Broadcasts

Ward-wise service announcements, tax reminders, scheme awareness, vaccination drives, public notices and event updates.

Example: K/East Ward — property tax payment reminder with WhatsApp payment link.

📈 Trend Detection

Early warning when complaint categories spike — potholes, drains, garbage, water supply, streetlights, illegal hoardings.

Example: Pothole complaints spike after heavy rainfall in a ward cluster.
