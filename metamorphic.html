The Urban Reality

Cities Are Suffocating.
The Ground Has No Room for Trees.

Modern urban centres face extreme air pollution, shrinking green cover, and zero space for traditional nature-based solutions. The infrastructure gap demands a new approach.

Space Scarcity

Dense urban land is fully consumed by buildings, roads, and transit corridors. Large-scale tree plantations are structurally impossible.

Continuous Pollution

Vehicular emissions, industrial VOCs, and PM2.5 particulates are generated around the clock without pause or seasonal rest.

Climate Urgency

Cities need measurable, visible, and immediately deployable climate interventions — not distant promises or decade-long timelines.

The Solution

A Living Climate Asset
for Urban Ground Zero

Metamorphic deploys biological carbon fixation through algae, combined with self-powered air circulation, to act as a continuously operating urban air rejuvenation system.

  • Bioactive Photobioreactor Core

    High-density algae cultivation converts CO₂ into oxygen through natural photosynthesis — continuously and silently.

  • Self-Powered Operation

    An integrated vertical-axis wind turbine generates the energy required for essential circulation — no grid dependency.

  • Urban-Compatible Design

    Engineered for pavements, corporate campuses, transit hubs, and dense urban corridors where space is critically limited.

Bioactive Core
How It Works

From Polluted Air to
Biological Value

A continuous, autonomous cycle that transforms urban atmospheric pollutants into clean oxygen and valuable biomass.

01

Air Intake

Polluted urban air is drawn into the system through passive and wind-assisted intake.

02

Biological Fixation

Algae cultures within the photobioreactor absorb CO₂ and volatile pollutants naturally.

03

Oxygen Release

Photosynthesis converts captured carbon into breathable oxygen, released back into the environment.

04

Biomass Yield

Captured carbon is transformed into biological biomass with potential secondary applications.
