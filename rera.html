Core Infrastructure Components

  • Cryptographic hash registry ensuring certificate provenance.
  • Unique QR plate deployment with anti-tamper serialization.
  • AI-driven WhatsApp integration (NLU + retrieval augmentation).
  • Dynamic property intelligence micro-sites.
  • Unified engagement and conversion API gateway.

Advanced Intelligence Layer

  • RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) over verified disclosures.
  • Automated buyer persona inference and qualification matrices.
  • Adaptive dialogue trees triggered by real-time intent signals.
  • Predictive funnel stage probability scoring.
  • Anomaly detection for fraud and misuse typologies.

Conversational Journey Orchestration

  1. Scan Initiation: QR triggers verified landing handoff or deep links to WhatsApp with secure session token.
  2. Verification Greeting: Chatbot delivers dynamic trust capsule (project snapshot + cryptographic hash).
  3. Adaptive Qualification: System identifies budget, timeline, and use-case (investment vs. end-user) via natural language.
  4. Data Structuring: Intent vectors are generated; scoring pipeline pushes updates to CRM in real-time.
  5. Asset Delivery: Personalized collateral (floor plans, pricing bands) is dispatched with monitored engagement telemetry.

Design Principle: Zero Re-Keying

Every interaction yields structured, attributable data that augments a centralized market intelligence graph, establishing a self-reinforcing learning loop for enterprise deployment.
