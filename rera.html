  • WhatsApp chatbot triggered on QR scan (deep-link integration)
  • AI-driven Q&A using MahaRERA's verified project data (RAG)
  • Structured citizen intent capture & lead scoring engine
  • Developer-facing CRM dashboard with real-time lead alerts

Citizen Conversation Flow — What the Buyer Experiences

  1. Scan QR at project site / in advertisement → WhatsApp opens with pre-loaded project context.
  2. AI greets with trust capsule: "This project is MahaRERA verified ✓ — Registration P51234567890. Here's what I can help you with."
  3. Guided Q&A: Floor plans, pricing range, completion timeline, RERA compliance status, builder track record — all from verified data.
  4. Intent capture: "Are you looking to buy for self-use or investment? What's your budget range? When are you planning to purchase?"
  5. Handoff: Qualified lead with full context pushed to developer's CRM. Citizen receives personalized brochure + site visit link.

Technical Principle

The AI layer reads from MahaRERA's existing project database via API. It does not modify, store, or duplicate any regulatory data. All citizen conversations are consent-based, PII-compliant, and auditable. WoW‑Strategies operates as a licensed technology partner — not a data controller.
