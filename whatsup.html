Appendix D: Financial Model Template

Revenue Calculation Formulas

Monthly Revenue Components:

Convenience Fees:
Revenue = Σ(Transaction_Amount × Fee_Percentage × Transaction_Count)
Example: ₹1,000 × 1.5% × 1000 transactions = ₹15,000

Vendor Subscriptions:
Revenue = Σ(Vendor_Count_by_Tier × Monthly_Fee_by_Tier)
Example: (100 × ₹500) + (50 × ₹2,500) + (10 × ₹15,000) = ₹325,000

Pay-per-Lead:
Revenue = Σ(Leads_Generated × Price_per_Lead_by_Category)
Example: (200 × ₹100) + (50 × ₹500) = ₹45,000

Advertising:
Revenue = (Impressions × CPM / 1000) + (Clicks × CPC)
Example: (100,000 × ₹200/1000) + (500 × ₹10) = ₹25,000

Total Monthly Revenue = Sum of all components
Annual Revenue = Monthly Revenue × 12 × Growth_Factor
